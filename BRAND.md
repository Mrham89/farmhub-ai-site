# AnImus Labs — Brand Essence & Mascot Bible

> Source-of-truth for the AnImus Labs identity. The mascot — **Argos** — is the
> reference for the company's *essence*: voice, values, and the one picture that
> says what we are. Slogans, page copy, and art all derive from this doc.

---

## The thesis (the whole company in one line)

**Private AI that runs on sunshine and answers only to your team — on hardware you own, off the grid, off the cloud.**

AnImus Labs is the **opposite of the Big-AI datacenter**: no surveillance, no monthly cloud bill, no "rent your own data back forever." Just capable AI that lives in *your* shop and serves *your* crew.

---

## Meet Argos — the mascot

**Argos** is a friendly **solar calculator brought to life** — the engineer's everyday tool, animated as the tireless sidekick.

He is built entirely of computer parts:
- **Solar-panel ball-cap** — his power comes from the sun (off-grid is native to a calculator).
- **LCD screen face** — the glowing smile; he's alive and on your side.
- **Calculator keypad body** — the engineer's trusted tool.
- **Cable arms** — one hand a **USB memory stick**, one a **DDR5 RAM stick**: he carries your *data*.
- **Computer-mouse feet** — he walks to wherever your team needs him.

### The story (where Argos comes from)
The inspiration is Disney *Fantasia* / the Sorcerer's Apprentice — the **enchanted broom that tirelessly hauls water in buckets**. That scene is two metaphors, and each has a computer-world translation:

| Fantasia | Argos |
|---|---|
| The **broom** (an everyday object brought to life to do the work) | a **computer/tool brought to life** — walking on wire legs, cable hands |
| The **water** it hauls (the vital resource) | **data** — carried as memory sticks & chips |

So Argos is the apprentice's broom, reborn as the engineer's helper: instead of hauling water, he hauls and guards your **data**.

### The name
**Argos** — Odysseus's faithful dog, the most *loyal* companion in Greek myth (he waited twenty years and recognized his master at a glance). The name also echoes **Argus**, the ever-watchful. It fits both halves of AnImus: the **loyal sidekick** and the **vigilant guardian** of your data. (Mythic kinship: Hephaestus, the outcast inventor-god who forged living automatons — the engineer who brings tools to life is, quite literally, what AnImus Labs does.)

## Archetype (Jungian — embedded in Argos)

| Role | Archetype | What it gives us | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | **The Caregiver** | Argos exists to help and protect his crew — make the team's work lighter. | Warm, dependable, tireless |
| **Secondary** | **The Outlaw / Rebel** | He broke free from the cloud; rejects the Big-AI datacenter bargain. | Quietly defiant, principled |
| **Grounding** | **The Everyman** | A humble pocket calculator — one of the crew, plain-spoken. | Down-to-earth, no jargon |

**Essence in one line:** *the loyal helper who runs on sunshine and keeps your data yours.*

**Tone rules:** warm first, defiant second (we're *for* your team more than *against* the cloud); plain-spoken (no enterprise word-salad); principled, never preachy.
